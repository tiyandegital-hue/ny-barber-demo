# Niche research — barbershop websites (2026-10-25)

Rule applied: niche research FIRST, before redesigning (Tanjir's order 2026-10-25).
Sources: listicles (CyberOptik 20 best, Zarla 15 best 2026, Colorlib, GlossGenius,
Vandelay 27, Buildrok barber template, Rocket "Fade" template) + 3 real sites
opened and read directly: Rudy's Barbershop, Pall Mall Barbers NYC, Gentlemen's Tonic.

## Patterns found across the best barbershop sites

1. **Photo-first hero, not abstract graphics.** Rudy's, Fort Worth, Buildrok
   "Heritage" all lead with REAL photos of cuts / the shop itself, full-width,
   with a motto + CTA on top. "Show the cuts, not a stock photo of scissors."
   Implication for us: our 3D pole must NOT lead the hero anymore. Hero becomes
   photography-led (our licensed Commons photos); the pole drops to a smaller
   animated accent in a craft section.
2. **Booking CTA everywhere + sticky on phones.** Multiple booking CTAs down the
   page; Rocket template: sticky bottom "Book Your Chair" bar after first scroll;
   Buildrok: a call button that stays on screen on phones. Implication: add a
   sticky mobile booking bar (Book button + tap-to-call) and repeat CTAs.
3. **Price menu board.** Every strong site shows exact prices + durations in a
   menu-board layout (dotted leaders, price right-aligned), not hidden behind
   clicks. Implication: Services page becomes a menu board grouped by category,
   with a photo header per category.
4. **First-timer reassurance / FAQ.** Pall Mall's FAQ answers: what to expect,
   walk-ins?, suitable for first-timers?, corporate-fit? Implication: add a
   "First visit?" block + FAQ on Visit — BUT only verified facts (no walk-in
   claim for AoP: unverified. Answer: what happens, durations, prices,
   Saturday closed, demo note).
5. **One consistent premium register: dark + gold/brass.** Imperium (black+gold
   regal), SHED (black-white + red accent), Fort Worth (black + white type),
   Hair-and-There dark urban template. Implication: our dark + brass glass
   system already matches the niche register — KEEP it, don't lighten.
6. **Gallery of finished work on Home.** "Show the cuts" — gallery strips of
   finished cuts, not just service lists. Implication: photo gallery strip on
   Home using our 5 licensed photos.
7. **Sticky header.** Mr Hobbs, Abels: nav follows scroll. Already have it — keep.
8. **City in the headline claim — WITHOUT unverified superlatives.** Zarla's
   steal: "Toronto's best cuts" beats generic "best cuts". Our honesty law
   forbids "#1/best" without basis, so we adopt the pattern, not the claim:
   "A sharp cut in Midtown, taken slowly." (place-specific, no superlative).
9. **Dark gallery backgrounds make photos look premium.** Gentlemen's dark
   gallery sections. Implication: gallery on dark glass, not light cards.

## Deliberately NOT adopted (honesty law)

- Testimonials / review sections — would have to be invented for AoP. Skipped.
- Barber profile cards — would invent people. Skipped.
- "Award-winning / #1 / best" claims — unverified for AoP. Skipped.
- Newsletter popups, shop/merch sections — fake signup / no real products. Skipped.
- Walk-ins welcome — AoP walk-in policy unverified. Skipped.

## Redesign direction (from this research)

Dark glass system stays (niche-confirmed). Structure changes to the niche's
winning shape: photo-led hero + motto + dual CTA → gallery strip → menu-board
services preview → first-visit reassurance → visit/hours → repeated booking
CTAs + sticky mobile booking bar. 3D pole survives as a craft accent, not the
hero lead.
